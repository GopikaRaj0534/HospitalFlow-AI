Medical Safety & Clinical Governance

HospitalFlow AI is a clinical decision-support and patient flow optimization system. The AI Symptom Checker provides preliminary informational screenings based on trained machine-learning patterns and does NOT substitute for qualified medical diagnosis, emergency services, or professional healthcare advice.

If you experience acute chest pain, severe shortness of breath, sudden numbness, or loss of consciousness, seek immediate emergency medical care or call 911 / emergency services directly.
